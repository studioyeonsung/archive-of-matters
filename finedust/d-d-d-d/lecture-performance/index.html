D: D-D-D

2023 Lecture Performance Collaboration with Mina Kim Live sensing, projection, sound mix
  • Fine Dust
  • Lecture Performance
  • D: D-D-D
Completed

〈D: D-D-D〉 is a lecture performance that treats fine dust as something that can be spoken, worn, and staged. Two performers in red and blue—wearing sensing helmets—translate urban particulate matter into image, sound, and gesture.

Street footage is marked with pixelated dust, then heated into a thermal field. The talk does not stand apart from the weather it describes: the audience sits on the floor while a city street becomes a live mix of data, body, and voice.
