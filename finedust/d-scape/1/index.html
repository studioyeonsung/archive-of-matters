D-Scape 1.0

  • Fine Dust
  • Scenography
  • D-Scape
Completed

The <D-Scene> series is the prototype of a constantly changing Dust-Data-Landscape working with real-time fine dust data.

The first prototype, <D-Scene 1.0> is an attempt to materialize the transboundary geopolitics of fine dust by creating a constantly changing atmosphere which is correlated with Asian or Yellow Dust, using local Dutch PM2.5 data transmitted from the <D-Beacon 1.0> to the exhibition place.

Programming, software development
