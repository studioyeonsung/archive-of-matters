DUST WEATHER MATTER(S)
COPPER The conductor of colonial capital, still vibrating across bodies and empires.

Copper, the first matter explored within The Matter Trilogy, serves not merely as a material, but as an active conductor—transmitting the logics of colonial capitalism across eras and geographies.

In the project Rusty Odyssey, copper becomes a protagonist that rewrites Eurocentric historical narratives, tracing the intertwined routes of the Netherlands, Japan, and Korea into an alternative cartography of inherited colonial capitalism.

This project grounds itself on a layered foundation of research essays, reimagining copper not simply as a mineral but as a human agent animated by capital's circulations. Copper, thus, becomes a medium through which decolonial discourse is not represented, but experimentally re-formed.

The practice traverses multiple registers—experimental documentary, installation, and textual research—each unfolding different sensory, material, and political dimensions of copper. Through this multi-modal engagement, Rusty Odyssey writes a re-sensing of history: one in which matter itself remembers, carries, and resists inherited violence.
